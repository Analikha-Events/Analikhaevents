The Founder

Deepesha S G

Analikha Events is envisioned as a platform where celebrations meet creativity, culture and meaningful human connection.

Founded and curated by Deepesha S G, the brand focuses on bringing together skilled artists, performers and event professionals to create bespoke experiences for clients.

"Every celebration has a story. Our role is to curate the moments that make it unforgettable."
